# Permissions declaration

## ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Purpose: calculate real-time GPS speed, distance, heading, altitude and trip statistics. NoxSpeed does not request background location in v1.0.

Suggested Play explanation (EN):
"Location is required while the app is in use to calculate GPS speed, distance, heading and trip statistics. NoxSpeed does not request background location."

Suggested Play explanation (ES):
"La ubicación es necesaria mientras se usa la aplicación para calcular velocidad GPS, distancia, rumbo y estadísticas del viaje. NoxSpeed no solicita ubicación en segundo plano."

## INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Used by Google Mobile Ads, UMP consent and Google Play Billing connectivity.

## VIBRATE
Optional haptic feedback and speed-alert vibration.
