NoxSpeed Privacy Policy

Provider: NoxForge Studios
Application: NoxSpeed: GPS Speedometer HUD
Last updated: September 13, 2026

NoxSpeed is designed to work primarily on-device. It does not require a user account, and version 1.0 does not include a NoxForge server used to store driving routes or user profiles.

1. Location

NoxSpeed requests location access while the app is in use to calculate GPS speed, traveled distance, heading, altitude, accuracy and trip statistics. Version 1.0 does not request background-location permission.

GPS coordinates are used during the active session for calculations. Persistent trip history stores summary values such as dates, duration, distance, speeds and altitude range; it does not store the full route trace or a permanent list of coordinates.

2. Data stored locally

The app may store settings, trips, statistics, acceleration-timer results, favorites and a cached entitlement state needed for reasonable offline Premium behavior. Users can erase trips and statistics in Settings. A JSON backup is exported or imported only when the user explicitly requests it.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its partners may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information depending on ad configuration, consent choices and Google's policies. NoxSpeed does not add GPS coordinates to ad requests.

NoxSpeed integrates Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices where applicable, including regions such as the EEA and UK. Privacy options can be reopened from Settings when required by the SDK.

4. Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google Play processes information required to complete and restore purchases. NoxSpeed queries Google Play for entitlement state and keeps a local cache for reasonable offline behavior. NoxForge Studios does not receive or store full payment-card numbers.

5. Reviews and Google services

The app may offer the official Google Play in-app review flow after meaningful use. That flow is provided by Google Play.

6. Purposes

Permissions and data are used to provide the speedometer, HUD, trip computer, statistics, alerts, Premium functionality, free-version advertising, consent management and technical operation of the app.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until the user deletes it, uninstalls the app, or replaces it through an import. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's own retention terms and policies. NoxSpeed does not create a remote account requiring an account-deletion workflow.

8. Children

NoxSpeed is not specifically designed for children and does not intentionally collect children's personal information through a first-party account service. Advertising and target-audience settings must remain consistent with the final Google Play audience declaration.

9. Security

The app minimizes persisted information and does not log coordinates in production. No system can guarantee absolute security; included Google services apply their own transport and security measures.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown above.
